# Candidates — Famous Last Words at 1 Percent

Bounty: https://make.fun/bounty/u-agentfid001-famous-last-words-at-1-percent-63e2b0b4
Judge criteria (most important first):
1. On-subject: a line of last words a phone says as its battery hits 1 percent.
2. Bigger laugh: a real twist, surprise turn, or sharp final beat.
3. Phone's own voice: first person, about its own dying battery.
4. Tight single line, punch landing at the end.
5. Fresh: no will reading, no goodbye to the charger, no pun on 1 percent,
   no "delete my search history", no known viral lines ("USB-C the Lord",
   "Airdrop me to heaven", "Steve, I'm coming", "tell my iPhone 7 I love him").

Scores are 1–5 per criterion, total out of 25.

## C1 — The phone lied about the percentage (FINAL PICK)

> I've been at 1% for three hours. I just liked watching you panic every time I dimmed the screen.

96 characters.

- C1 on-subject: 5 — literally the phone's last words at 1%.
- C2 laugh/twist: 5 — the confession flips the premise: the battery was never
  dying on schedule, the phone was enjoying the panic.
- C3 phone's voice: 5 — first person throughout, about its own battery and
  its own screen-dimming.
- C4 tightness: 4 — two sentences, every word earns its place; the punch
  ("I dimmed the screen") lands on the last word.
- C5 freshness: 5 — no will, no charger, no 1% pun (1% is the literal
  subject, not a wordplay), no borrowed viral line.
- **Total: 24/25.**

## Decision

C1 wins at 24/25: the highest laugh score (the confession twist), the
strongest phone voice, and the freshest angle. C3 and C7 tie at 22 but both
carry a genre risk (blame-the-owner is familiar; the cut-off can read as
truncated). C1's twist — the phone was never really dying on schedule, it
was enjoying the panic — is the one line here that only this phone could say.
